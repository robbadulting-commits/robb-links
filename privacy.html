Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

What this page collects

If you send an idea through the "What should I fix next?" form, we receive what you type: your idea, the room you picked and, if you add it, your social media handle. Nothing else is required. We use this only to choose and credit future episodes, and we never sell it or share it with brands.

Where it's stored

The site and the form are hosted by Netlify, which stores submissions for us. Netlify may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the service.

Links to other sites

Product links go to Amazon and Temu. Some are affiliate links: as an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. Once you leave this page, those sites' own privacy policies apply, and they may use cookies to track the referral.

Your choices

You don't have to use the form. If you've sent an idea and want it deleted, message @robbadulting on Instagram and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
